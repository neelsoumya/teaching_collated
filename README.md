# Teaching Materials by Soumya Banerjee

A curated index of public teaching repositories under [`neelsoumya`](https://github.com/neelsoumya), with links and brief descriptions. The descriptions are based on repository names, available README material, and repository pages; individual notebooks and linked resources may contain additional material.

## Large language models, generative AI, and AI safety

- **[Teaching LLM Applications](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teaching_llm_applications)** — MSc-level course on LLM internals and applications, with lecture notes and Python practicals. Topics include tokenisation, embeddings, attention, Transformers, pre-training and scaling, fine-tuning/RLHF, prompting, RAG, agents and tools, evaluation, safety, interpretability, DPO, efficient architectures, and reinforcement learning.
- **[Teaching LLM Agents](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teaching_llm_agents)** — Teaching resources on LLM-based agents, agent workflows, tool use, and related application patterns.
- **[AI Safety Teaching Resources](https://github.com/neelsoumya/AI_safety_teaching_resources)** — Course resources for introducing AI safety, AI ethics, and philosophy of AI, including a course PDF and further reading.
- **[Special Topics in Unconventional AI](https://github.com/neelsoumya/special_topics_unconventional_AI)** — Special-topics course on unconventional approaches to artificial intelligence, with a companion course website.
- **[Winter School in AI](https://github.com/neelsoumya/winter_school_AI)** — AI teaching and practical material, including introductory Transformer notebooks.
- **[AI Outreach / PPI Resources](https://github.com/neelsoumya/ai_outreach)** — Public-facing resources for explaining AI, especially AI in healthcare, to patients and the wider public. Includes interactive explainers, browser-based model-building tools, responsible-AI resources, and material on patient and public involvement.
- **[Outreach and PPI (original repository)](https://github.com/neelsoumya/outreach_ppi)** — Earlier repository associated with public engagement and patient/public involvement resources for trustworthy AI.
- **[AI for Biomedical Students](https://github.com/neelsoumya/AI_for_biomedical_students)** — AI and machine-learning teaching resources aimed at biomedical and life-science students.
- **[AI for School Students](https://github.com/neelsoumya/AI_for_school_students)** — Introductory AI material for school-age learners.
- **[Public Talk: AI in India](https://github.com/neelsoumya/public_talk_AI_India)** — Material supporting a public-facing talk about AI in the Indian context.
- **[IITM Generative AI](https://github.com/neelsoumya/IITM_GenAI)** — Generative-AI teaching material associated with IIT Madras.
- **[Teaching Neural Network Animation](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teaching_neural_network_animation)** — Small repository for teaching neural networks through animation or visual explanation.

## Machine learning, deep learning, and neural networks

- **[Python Machine Learning](https://github.com/neelsoumya/python_machine_learning)** — Broad Python-based ML teaching collection with notebooks, notes, code, and data. Covers Python basics, supervised and unsupervised learning, PCA, t-SNE, embeddings, feature engineering, neural networks, backpropagation, and applied examples including healthcare data.
- **[Public Teaching: Unsupervised Learning](https://github.com/neelsoumya/public_teaching_unsupervised_learning)** — Teaching notes and practical resources focused on unsupervised machine learning and dimensionality reduction.
- **[Teaching Neural Networks](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teaching_neural_networks)** — Beginner-friendly neural-network and CNN course, with visual explanations, practical code, and biological applications such as microscopy, cell classification, and medical imaging.
- **[Introduction to PyTorch](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teaching_intro_pytorch)** — Hands-on introduction to tensors, autograd, forward/backward passes, linear regression, and simple neural networks.
- **[Butterfly Detector](https://github.com/neelsoumya/butterfly_detector)** — Collection of deep-learning tutorials and notebooks, including image classification, Fashion-MNIST, variational autoencoders, graph neural networks, reinforcement-learning interfaces, and neural cellular automata.
- **[Visualization Lecture](https://github.com/neelsoumya/visualization_lecture)** — Notes, slides, code, and datasets for data visualization, dimensionality reduction, and the mathematics of machine learning. Includes Python exercises and material on communicating data through visual narratives.
- **[Linear Mixed-Effects Model Teaching](https://github.com/neelsoumya/linear_mixed_effects_model_teaching)** — Teaching examples and resources for linear mixed-effects models and statistical modelling.
- **[Complex Systems Teaching Resources](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teaching_resource_complex_systems)** — Repository for teaching material on complex systems.
- **[Mathematics](https://github.com/neelsoumya/mathematics)** — Small repository of mathematics-related teaching or learning material; inspect the files for the current contents.

## Robotics and navigation

- **[Teaching Robot Navigation](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teaching_robots_navigation)** — Robotics course notes and practicals covering ROS/ROS 2, Gazebo/RViz, odometry, localisation, mapping, SLAM, path planning, autonomous navigation, indoor drones, cooperative robots, safety and ethics, and connections between LLMs and robotics. Includes practical examples and course links.
- **[Mumbai Teaching](https://github.com/neelsoumya/mumbai_teaching)** — Teaching material associated with Mumbai; see the repository for the specific course and resources.
- **[Wuhan COVID Scavenger Hunt (Streamlit)](https://github.com/neelsoumya/wuhan_covid_scavenger_hunt_streamlit_teaching)** — Interactive Streamlit-based teaching activity using a scavenger-hunt format around COVID-related information.

## Programming, web development, reproducibility, and data science practice

- **[Teaching Web Development](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teaching_web_development)** — Introductory full-stack web-development material, including HTML/CSS, PHP, forms, databases, authentication, security practices, labs, and assessment ideas.
- **[Teaching Reproducible Science in R](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teaching_reproducible_science_R)** — Resources for reproducible computational research using R and R Markdown, including documented analysis workflows, exercises, slides, and examples of reproducible projects.
- **[Teaching R Markdown and Git](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teaching_rmarkdown_git)** — Compact collection of resources for teaching R Markdown, Git, and reproducibility, with links to training material and related repositories.
- **[Getting Started with Data Science](https://github.com/neelsoumya/getting-_started_data_science)** — Introductory resources for getting started with data science; check the repository contents for its current lesson structure.
- **[Teaching Big Data Analytics](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teaching_big_data_analytics)** — Teaching repository for big-data analytics.
- **[Teaching Resources](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teaching_resources)** — General compilation of teaching resources across data science, machine learning, bioinformatics, and biology.
- **[Theory of Computer Science Teaching](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teaching_theory_computer_science)** — Teaching resources for theoretical computer science.
- **[Teachable Machine Community](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teachablemachine-community)** — Community-oriented material related to browser-based, accessible machine-learning education.
- **[Teachable Machine v1](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teachable-machine-v1)** — Code/resources related to an early Teachable Machine project for interactive, browser-based machine learning.

## Broader open-source collections with teaching material

- **[Public Open-Source Data Science](https://github.com/neelsoumya/public_open_source_data_science)** — Larger catalogue of open-source data-science projects, including resources for AI for social good, biology, clinical informatics, NLP, LLMs, browser-based data science, and public-facing AI education.
- **[AI Autograder Project](https://github.com/neelsoumya/ai_autograder_project)** — Project repository exploring AI-supported grading or assessment workflows.
- **[Working with Domain Experts](https://github.com/neelsoumya/working_with_domain_experts)** — Small repository related to interdisciplinary collaboration and working with subject-matter experts; inspect its files for details.

3. [Robot Navigation](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teaching_robots_navigation) — robotics, navigation, SLAM, and practicals.
4. [AI Safety Teaching Resources](https://github.com/neelsoumya/AI_safety_teaching_resources) — AI safety and ethics.
5. [Teaching Neural Networks](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teaching_neural_networks) — accessible deep-learning material with biological examples.
6. [Reproducible Science in R](https://github.com/neelsoumya/teaching_reproducible_science_R) — reproducible workflows and research practice.
7. [Visualization Lecture](https://github.com/neelsoumya/visualization_lecture) — data visualization and dimensionality reduction.
8. [AI Outreach](https://github.com/neelsoumya/ai_outreach) — public engagement and trustworthy AI.
